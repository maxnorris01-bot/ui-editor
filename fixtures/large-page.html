Questions, answered

How long is the free trial?

Every plan starts with a 14 day trial. No card is needed until you decide to keep going, and you can export your data at any time.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period, so you never pay twice.

Do you offer discounts for students?

Students and teachers get 50 percent off any plan. Send us a message from your school address and we will set it up.

Where is my data stored?

Your projects live in data centres in the EU and the US. You can choose a region when you create your workspace.

Is there an API?

There is a REST API on the Team and Studio plans, with webhooks for the events you are most likely to care about.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing in your account settings and choose Cancel. Your workspace stays readable for 30 days afterwards.
